Needs your OK · Colour and type · Photos on the plan

Plan

  • Rounded system font throughout the app.
  • Each meal type has its own colour: breakfast orange, lunch teal, dinner indigo.
  • A small photo on every planned meal. Empty slots show a dashed "+" in the meal type's colour.
  • A Tonight card at the top of the current week. The plan always opens at Monday now, so this keeps today's dinner in view.
  • Today's section gets a green outline and a TODAY tag.

Shopping list

  • Each aisle gets its own coloured icon: carrot, fish, milk, croissant, tin, house.
  • A bolder progress bar that also says how many items are left.
  • Ticks pop when you check an item off.
  • When everything is ticked, an "All done!" card replaces the progress bar.
Needs your OK · Friendlier words · Little moments

Empty screens

  • Warmer wording and a clear main button, so an empty screen says what to do next.
  • A new "blank week" card on an empty Plan week, with Randomise Week and Pick a Meal.
  • The dice bounce whenever you randomise.
Already agreed · spec v1.6 (M12.2)

Settings, sharing and the keyboard

  • Settings is a list of rows. Each row shows its current value and opens its own page.
  • Reminder and Sharing each get their own page. The WhatsApp contact becomes an optional quick-send contact.
  • With a contact saved, the share button offers "Send to Sam" and "Share…". Without one, it opens the iOS share sheet straight away.
  • Number keypads get a Done key. Tapping outside a field or scrolling also hides the keyboard.
  • The coloured icon tiles on the Settings rows are part of the "Colour and type" idea.
